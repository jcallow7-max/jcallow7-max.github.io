Hours

Regular Hours

Jazzy Cougar opens around midday and stays open into the evening, giving customers plenty of time to browse without rushing. Our later weekend hours are perfect for selecting a cigar, restocking a favorite blend, or finding the right accessory before the night begins.

  • Monday–Thursday: 12:00 p.m.–9:00 p.m.
  • Friday–Saturday: 12:00 p.m.–11:00 p.m.
  • Sunday: 1:00 p.m.–7:00 p.m.

Hours may change on holidays or during private events. Customers planning a late visit are encouraged to contact their nearest Jazzy Cougar location before heading out, because even JC eventually takes off the hat and clocks out.
