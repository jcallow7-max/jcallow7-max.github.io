Welcome to Jazzy Cougar

Jazzy Cougar is a specialty tobacco shop for those with a taste for craftsmanship, character, and a little unnecessary sophistication. From hand-rolled cigarettes and cigars to carefully selected tobacco products and accessories, everything we offer is chosen with attention to quality and presentation.

Step inside and take your time. Explore our products, discover the story behind Jazzy Cougar, and learn more about the standards that shape what we make and sell. Around here, ordinary has never been part of the selection.
