Jazzy Cougar Creative Tech Hub

Build. Create. Repair. Improve.

The Jazzy Cougar Creative Tech Hub is a themed workspace for students and creators who want to learn web development, produce music, repair technology, and stay disciplined with their goals.

Members can use the hub to work on personal projects, build technical skills, and connect creative ideas with real-world tools.

What the Hub Offers

  • Web development practice using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
  • Music production resources for beats, recording, and editing
  • Computer repair and refurbishment tools
  • Fitness and discipline planning resources
  • Project workspaces for creative technology ideas

Why Join?

The goal of the Jazzy Cougar Creative Tech Hub is to give students a place to combine creativity, technology, discipline, and practical skills. Instead of focusing on only one area, the hub connects multiple interests into one organized learning space.
